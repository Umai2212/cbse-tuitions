Our Method

How Does Elite IIT Help Students Improve in Weak Subjects?

A student may attend regular classes and still lose marks because of unclear basics, incomplete practice, poor answer presentation, or unresolved doubts. Elite IIT’s CBSE program follows a structured improvement process.

1

Step 1: Diagnostic Assessment

We begin by understanding the student’s current level. A diagnostic assessment helps identify weak chapters, recurring mistakes, and subject-specific gaps.

2

Step 2: Concept Rebuilding

Once weak areas are identified, faculty revisit fundamentals using simple explanations, worked examples, and NCERT-aligned practice.

3

Step 3: Exam-Pattern Practice

Students practise school-exam questions, previous-year questions, sample papers, and application-based questions. This helps them learn how to apply concepts under exam conditions.

4

Step 4: Test Analysis and Revision Plan

After every test, students receive feedback on their mistakes. Faculty help them understand why marks were lost and create a focused revision plan.

How Do We Improve CBSE Maths Scores?

For Maths, we focus on concept clarity first, followed by NCERT practice, step-marking habits, and targeted revision of weak chapters. Students learn how to present calculations clearly and avoid avoidable mistakes.

How Do We Prepare Students for CBSE Science?

For Science, we cover Physics numericals, Chemistry equations and reactions, Biology diagrams, definitions, and NCERT-based reasoning. Students practise application-based questions, not just theory.

How Do We Support Students Who Need Extra Help?

Students who need additional support can attend doubt sessions, revise weak chapters, and receive a personalised improvement plan based on test performance.
